# Northstar Fitness Collective — Brand Kits

A fictional multi-brand fitness franchisor for the **ApostropheCMS Assembly** demo. One parent organization runs a corporate site plus nine studio-concept brands, each its own tenant with its own domain, theme, and content — all managed from a single Assembly dashboard.

Every brand below is invented. Any resemblance to a real company is coincidental.

---

## Parent — Northstar Fitness Collective

The franchisor. Owns the concepts, licenses them to studio operators, and runs the corporate/investor/franchising site.

- **Tagline:** Nine ways to move. One collective.
- **One-liner:** A collective of specialized studio brands, each built around one thing done exceptionally well.
- **Palette:** Indigo `#1E2A5A` · Star gold `#F2C14E` · Mist `#B9C2E6` · Paper `#FFFFFF`
- **Fonts:** Headings — Söhne / Helvetica Neue Bold; Body — Inter / system sans
- **Personality:** Confident, structural, quietly premium. The steady hand behind the brands.
- **Logo file:** `logos/00-northstar-collective.svg`

### How this maps to Assembly

Each brand is a **tenant**: its own domain, theme tokens (the palette + fonts above), navigation, and content — spun up from a shared codebase and shared widget library. The Northstar corporate site is a tenant too. New location or new concept = new tenant, not a new codebase. That's the story the demo is telling.
